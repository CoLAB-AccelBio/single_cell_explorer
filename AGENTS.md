# Architecture rules
- Keep processing in the browser; uploaded measurements must never reach a server.
- Bind cached loads to a captured source and generation; consumer cancellation must not abort other consumers.
- Validate core data before use and verify positional expression files against the checksum declared by their matching core; legacy built-in assets require a pinned ordered-cell fingerprint and checksum.
- Use shared expression selectors for filtered populations and statistics; imported differential-expression results are invalidated by membership changes.
- Keep dormant illustrative analyses out of the explorer until validated scientific methods are available.
- Use semantic visualization tokens for drawing and clearly identify raster export formats.
